Industry Performance Report 2025

Industry Performance Report 2025

The MCMC Industry Performance Report 2025 offers a comprehensive view of Malaysia's communications and multimedia landscape, from the pace of 5G adoption and fibre expansion to the evolving digital habits shaping the country's connectivity, economy and everyday life.

In 2025, Malaysia's communications sector continued to drive growth through stronger mobile and fixed broadband coverage, improved network resilience and rising demand for cloud, cybersecurity and data analytics. The report examines the performance of key services, tracks emerging trends in digital infrastructure and highlights the opportunities created by faster, more reliable connectivity across households, businesses and communities.

Executive Summary

Malaysia's communications and multimedia industry remained resilient in 2025, supported by sustained demand for connectivity, continued infrastructure investment and the growing role of digital services in everyday life. Listed communications and multimedia companies recorded RM110.63 billion in market capitalisation, while industry revenue grew by 1.5% to RM49.45 billion. National connectivity also advanced, with 9.81 million premises passed with fibre, 99.71% 4G LTE coverage in populated areas and 29.0 million 5G subscriptions.

Malaysia's communications and multimedia (C&M) industry remained resilient in 2025, supported by sustained demand for digital connectivity and infrastructure investment. Industry revenue increased by 1.5%, while listed C&M companies continued to play an important role in Malaysia's digital economy, with telecommunications remaining the leading contributor.

  • RM49.45B C&M Industry Revenue
  • RM110.63B Listed C&M Market Capitalisation

Malaysia continued to strengthen its digital connectivity in 2025 through the expansion of broadband, fibre and mobile infrastructure under national initiatives such as JENDELA. Fibre connectivity reached 9.81 million premises passed, exceeding the national target of 9 million, while 4G LTE coverage expanded to 99.71% of populated areas. The country also recorded 29.0 million 5G subscriptions, reflecting growing adoption of next-generation mobile technology.

  • 9.81 Million Premises Passed With Fibre
  • 29.0 Million 5G Subscriptions

Malaysia's content services landscape continued to evolve as audiences increasingly embraced streaming, digital media and on-demand entertainment. While Pay-TV penetration declined amid changing viewing preferences, radio maintained a strong audience presence. Broadcasters also expanded their focus on local content, digital distribution and audience engagement.

  • 62.9% Pay-TV Penetration
  • 21.9 Million Weekly Radio Listeners

Malaysia continued advancing digital inclusion and trusted online services through community-based initiatives, digital authentication and growing participation in the digital economy. NADI expanded access to digital learning and services, while the increasing use of digital certificates supported secure online communications and transactions.

  • 1.9 Million+ NADI Users Nationwide
  • 34.6 Million Digital Certificates Issued Cumulatively
Explore Online and Community Services

Malaysia's postal and courier industry continued to transform, supported by growing e-commerce activity and demand for parcel delivery. Courier traffic expanded significantly in 2025, while parcel deliveries per capita exceeded the national PAKEJ target. Industry players also strengthened their infrastructure and adopted technology to improve delivery efficiency.

  • 45.9 Parcels Per Capita
  • 1.58 Billion Courier Items Handled
Explore Postal and Courier

Consumer protection, service quality and regulatory compliance remained central to MCMC's priorities in 2025. Despite higher complaint volumes, the majority of cases were resolved by year-end. MCMC also strengthened regulatory oversight, service standards and digital safety initiatives to improve accountability and consumer confidence.

  • 96% Consumer Complaints Resolved
  • 71,919 Consumer Complaints Recorded
