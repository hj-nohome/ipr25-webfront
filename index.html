Industry Performance Report 2025

The MCMC Industry Performance Report 2025 offers a comprehensive view of Malaysia's communications and multimedia landscape, from the pace of 5G adoption and fibre expansion to the evolving digital habits shaping the country's connectivity, economy and everyday life.

In 2025, Malaysia's communications sector continued to drive growth through stronger mobile and fixed broadband coverage, improved network resilience and rising demand for cloud, cybersecurity and data analytics. The report examines the performance of key services, tracks emerging trends in digital infrastructure and highlights the oppotunities created by faster, more reliable connectivity across households, businesses and communities.

Executive Summary

Malaysia's communications and multimedia industry remained resilient in 2025, supported by sustained demand for connectivity, continued infrastructure investment and the growing role of digital services in everyday life.

Listed communications and multimedia companies recorded RM110.63 billion in market capitalisation, while industry revenue grew by 1.5% to RM49.45 billion. National connectivity also advanced, with 9.81 million premises passed with fibre, 99.71% 4G LTE coverage in populated areas and 29.0 million 5G subscriptions.

Across the wider ecosystem, digital inclusion, online safety, trusted transactions, content innovation and the continued growth of e-commerce and parcel delivery reinforced the industry's role in Malaysia's digital future. Read more
